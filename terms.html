What this site is

This is an informational website for the AgentPepe NFT collection. It does not connect wallets, hold funds, or execute transactions. All minting and claiming happens on BNB Chain through your own Binance agent and wallet, under your control.

Minting and claiming

  • Minting requires a Binance Agent ID in the minting wallet and passing an on-chain anti-bot question handled by your agent.
  • Prices, limits and fees are set by the smart contracts and shown in BNB. A claim fee of 0.00024 BNB applies per $APEPE claim.
  • Blockchain transactions are final. Check every address, amount and fee your agent shows you before confirming.
  • One claim per NFT token ID, ever. The NFT is not burned by claiming.

Availability

Mint and claim availability, counters and status shown on this site are read from public blockchain data and can lag or be interrupted. The smart contracts are the source of truth.
