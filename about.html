About AgentPepe

Last updated 11 November 2026

AgentPepe is a meme NFT collection of 6,666 glossy 3D frog agents on BNB Chain. It is built for users of Binance Agentic Wallet: every mint and every claim runs through your own agent, never through a button on this website.

How it works

  • Your wallet must hold a Binance Agent ID to mint, free or paid.
  • A first mint per wallet is free. Paid mints follow a simple ladder, up to 20 frogs per wallet.
  • Each frog gets its class from an on-chain seed at mint: Chill (about 70%), Degen (about 27%) or Golden (about 3%). Classes reveal 24 hours after mint.
  • Every frog claims $APEPE once per token ID: 250,000 $APEPE, or 2,500,000 for a Golden. Claiming never burns the NFT.
  • A holder snapshot is taken at every 500 mints.
