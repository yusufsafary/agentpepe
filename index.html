FAQ

Questions from the pond

When does minting open?

Minting is open now and the counter on this page is live. Claims for $APEPE are open too. Only trust addresses shown on this site.

Why can I only mint through an agent?

AgentPepe is built for Binance Agentic Wallet users. Minting runs through your agent, which checks your Agent ID, answers the gate question and signs the mint. This site never asks you to connect a wallet.

How does the free mint work?

Your first AgentPepe in each wallet costs 0 BNB in mint price. Your agent still needs a tiny amount of BNB for network gas, and a small commission can appear in the agent confirmation screen.

How do I know if I got a Golden Pepe?

Wait 24 hours after your mint. Classes come from the on-chain seed, roughly 70% Chill, 27% Degen and 3% Golden, and the reveal is automatic.

When can I claim $APEPE?

Claims are open now. Each NFT claims once: 250,000 $APEPE, or 2,500,000 for a Golden Pepe, for a claim fee of 0.00024 BNB. Claiming never burns your NFT.
